NASM Certification Policy – Terms & Conditions
1. Mandatory Certification
To provide Personal Training services within MTM Group, all fitness professionals must hold the required NASM certifications (CPT, PES, and CES) or provide evidence that they are actively enrolled and progressing towards completion in accordance with the MTM × NASM Certification Policy.
2. Recognition of Existing Certifications
Employees who already hold one or more of the required NASM certifications must provide an official copy of each certificate to the company.
Qualifications will only be recognized once satisfactory evidence has been submitted and verified. Failure to provide evidence will result in the qualification not being recognized, and the employee will be regarded as not holding the required certification.
3. Employees Holding Some Required Certifications
Employees who have already completed one or two of the required qualifications are not required to repeat those courses.
However, they will be required to purchase the remaining qualification at the bundle cost (QAR 1,825). This continues to represent a substantially discounted investment compared with the standard retail value of the individual courses.
4. Employees Missing Required Certifications
Employees who do not hold one or more of the required NASM certifications must enroll in and complete the outstanding qualifications through the MTM × NASM program. The cost again will be the bundle cost (QAR 1,825) which represents a substantially discounted investment compared with the standard retail value of the individual courses.
5. Employees Holding All Three Certifications
Employees who completed the NASM CPT, PES, and CES certifications before the implementation of the MTM × NASM Certification Policy will be exempt from any further financial contribution, provided official evidence of all three certifications is submitted and verified by the company.

6. Employees who fail the course or fail to complete it on time

If you fail the course you are obligated to pay the full bundle price of the course provided to MTM by NASM- this will represent an additional payment of QAR 1,825.
7. Two-Year Commitment
The MTM employee pricing has been made possible through a company supported investment in partnership with NASM.
Employees who choose not to commit to the agreed two year employment period must still enroll on the program; however, they will be required to pay the full MTM discounted program fee by covering the remaining subsidized amount (QAR 1,825) at the time of company exit.
8. Non-Compliance
Employees who are unable or unwilling to acknowledge or comply with the requirements outlined in this policy will be required to attend a meeting with the Human Resources Department and the Head of Personal Training to discuss the matter.
Where a resolution cannot be reached, the company reserves the right to determine the appropriate course of action in accordance with MTM Group policies, operational requirements, and the employee's contractual obligations. This may include restricting the employee from providing Personal Training services until the certification requirements have been satisfied.
